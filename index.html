About

A bit about me

I'm a Mechatronics and Cyber-Physical Systems master's student at the Deggendorf Institute of Technology, currently in my final semester. I started out in mechanical engineering, where my bachelor's thesis involved designing a soft robotic exosuit for the lower arm to help people with limited arm mobility. That project pulled me toward robotics and embedded systems, and I've been interested in robotics ever since.

My work spans ROS 2 and Gazebo simulation, autonomous navigation, embedded systems with ESP32/Arduino/STM32, and VR development in Unity.

I'm currently looking for master's thesis and working-student opportunities in mechatronics, robotics and embedded systems.
